Work out the monthly EMI

Banks in India work out a home-loan EMI from the loan amount (P), the monthly interest rate (r) and the number of monthly instalments (n):

EMI = P × r × (1+r)n ÷ ((1+r)n − 1)

Move the sliders and the figures update. The interest rate is only an example, so check your bank's current offer.
